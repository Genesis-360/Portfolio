## 9. Risk Mitigation

### Identified Risks

| Risk | Probability | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|-------------|--------|------------|
| **Client permission delays** for case studies | Medium | High | Start permission process early; use anonymized case studies initially |
| **Content creation bottlenecks** | Medium | Medium | Batch content creation; maintain editorial calendar; consider freelance writers |
| **Schema implementation errors** | Low | Medium | Use testing tools (Rich Results Test, Schema Validator); have developer review |
| **Algorithm updates** during critical period | Low | High | Follow Google Search Central blog; don't make major changes during known updates |
| **Budget constraints** | Medium | Medium | Phase implementation; focus on highest-ROI activities first |
| **Team availability** for bios/interviews | Medium | Medium | Schedule interviews in advance; prepare questionnaire template |
| **Competitor response** to SEO push | Low | Medium | Focus on own differentiation (AI-powered, performance-first); don't competitive-obsess |
| **Seasonal fluctuations** in B2B services | High | Low | Build evergreen content; don't rely on seasonal peaks; maintain consistent cadence |

### Contingency Plans

1. **If client permissions delayed**: Publish anonymized case studies ("A SaaS company", "Finance firm") with generic metrics; fill in specific details later.

2. **If content creation slow**: Reduce to 1 blog post per month initially; focus on quality over quantity; repurpose existing content.

3. **If schema errors found**: Use Google's Rich Results Test immediately; fix within 48 hours; maintain parallel manual submission.

4. **If traffic growth slower than expected**: Audit content quality; check technical SEO; review keyword selection; consider content gap analysis vs competitors.

5. **If algorithm update impacts**: Maintain calm; wait 2-4 weeks for stabilization; audit changes; adjust strategy based on new data.
